Our mission

We build and study technology so it protects people, children, animals, and ecosystems.

Responsible Systems Lab develops and studies practical technologies that help make complex systems safer, more humane, and more accountable. We focus on responsible AI, sensing, automation, verification, risk detection, and welfare-aware systems for real-world environments where technology affects people, communities, animals, and the environment.

We are an informal, interdisciplinary project group working on early-stage ideas, prototypes, and research-driven concepts — not an anti-technology campaign, and not a company selling a finished product. Our aim is technology that earns trust through transparency, human oversight, and practical risk reduction.

Initiated by Mohsen Seyedkazemi Ardebili, an independent AI platform and systems researcher. Explore the public engineering repositories and research identifier (ORCID) behind the work.

Active · released Privacy & accessibility · open source

YazSes

An open-source, offline voice-dictation daemon for Linux, macOS and Windows. Hold a key, speak, release — the speech is transcribed on your own CPU and typed into whatever window has focus. There is no network call in the dictation path, no account and no API key, so the privacy claim is one you can verify in the source rather than one you have to trust.

It includes a Dysfluency-Friendly Mode for stuttered or dysarthric speech and an EMG muscle-sensor trigger for hands-free use by people with motor disabilities — features that exist because they are worth doing, not because they enlarge a market.

Released beta AI agent evidence & replay

NovaFabric

Self-hosted, open-source infrastructure that captures AI agent executions as portable Run Capsules. It supports forensic inspection, supported mocked replay, structural comparison, provenance and optional sealing so a run can be examined after it finishes.

The responsibility question: can a team reproduce and inspect what an agent did without relying only on a hosted dashboard?

Research alpha Agent verification & human oversight

IDKMesh

An open-source research and engineering foundation for coordinating humans and AI agents around bounded work and independently checked results. Its current installable gate-audit command examines correlated verifier errors in AI review panels.

The responsibility question: when several reviewers approve an agent result, how independent and reliable is that approval?

Experimental Responsible distribution & approval

ReachWeaver

Experimental, offline-first tooling and a Claude Code skill for evidence-backed communications. It prepares channel-specific drafts, keeps human approval gates and can stage scheduled work; live publishing has not been validated with connected owner accounts.

The responsibility question: how can AI help communicate real work without fabricating claims, spamming communities or silently publishing?

Why responsible systems matter

Powerful systems need accountability designed in — not added later.

AI agents, automation, robotics, sensing platforms, and cloud systems are becoming part of everyday life. They can improve society, but they can also create risks when they are not designed with accountability, safety, welfare, and human dignity in mind. We focus on practical ways to make technology more transparent, verifiable, and aligned with real-world human and ecological needs.

Areas of focus

Where we work.

Five themes guide our projects.

Responsible AI & agentic systems

Designing AI and automated systems that stay understandable, accountable, and aligned with human needs and oversight.

Human dignity & safety

Building systems that protect people, respect rights and privacy, and reduce harm in real-world environments.

Child-safe technology

Exploring safeguards and design principles that help protect children in digital and automated systems.

Risk detection & verification

Developing practical methods to identify failures, verify processes, and support better, evidence-based decisions.

Ecosystem responsibility

Supporting technology that respects biodiversity, sustainability, and ecological resilience.
