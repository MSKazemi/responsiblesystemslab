Research alpha · independent verification

IDKMesh — Coordinate uncertain work, but verify results before accepting them.

IDKMesh is an open-source research and engineering foundation for WorkUnit contracts, provenance and independent review. The current installable gate-audit command measures correlated errors in verifier panels; the broader Verified Swarm Runner remains a research target.

Explore public source and experimentsRead the capability matrix

Why multiple reviewers are not enough

If AI reviewers make the same mistake, adding more votes may not increase reliability. IDKMesh studies how much independent evidence a review gate actually provides and reports known-bad candidates that passed.

Available work

Its public repository contains versioned task and verification contracts, local simulations and experiments, a read-only Control Tower for committed evidence reports, and a gate-audit CLI. The demos use synthetic fixtures; passing them is not proof of real-world correctness.

Research boundary

The system does not claim safe coordination of thousands of production machines. Its more ambitious multi-agent runner is incomplete, and independent-verifier contracts do not guarantee independent judgment in practice.
